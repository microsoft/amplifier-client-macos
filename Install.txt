Amplifier for Mac

1. Drag Amplifier.app to Applications.
2. Eject the Amplifier for Mac disk image.
3. Open Amplifier from Applications.
4. Enter your Amplifier host address, choose Connect in browser, and approve
   this Mac after comparing the code shown in the application.

You need an existing compatible Amplifier Unified host. Ask its owner for the
HTTPS address and sign-in access. Installing this app does not install a server.

This preview lives in the menu bar. Tempo is its optional desktop companion;
choose Hide Tempo if you only want the menu bar and notifications. The full
workspace opens in your browser or installed web app.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Liquid Glass is available on macOS 26 or later.
Official releases are signed and notarized. Check for Updates uses signed
updates; automatic checks are optional. No GitHub account is needed to download.

Help and downloads:
https://github.com/microsoft/amplifier-client-macos

To uninstall: disable Open Amplifier at login, revoke access through Manage
connected devices if desired, Forget the credential, quit Amplifier and remove
Amplifier.app. Saved messages and preferences remain on your Mac. See the
repository's connection and privacy guide for details.
